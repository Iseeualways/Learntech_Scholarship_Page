Questions, answered

Quick answers for students and parents.

Who can apply for these scholarships?

Students who meet each university's minimum marks. Symbiosis Dubai asks for 55% for Bachelor's and 50% for Master's. BITS Pilani Dubai asks for 60% for both Bachelor's and Master's. A counsellor confirms what you qualify for.

What is the deadline?

Scholarship windows are limited and vary by intake, so apply early. Share your details and a counsellor will confirm the exact date for your university.

Can I apply for a Master's programme?

Yes. Symbiosis Dubai offers 35% on the full programme plus AED 1,000, from 50% marks. BITS Pilani Dubai offers 20% off first-semester tuition on ME and MBA with 75%+ in your degree. You need 60% to apply.

Does the scholarship apply to every course?

Percentages are set by university and level. At Symbiosis Dubai, Bachelor's courses get 40% in year 1 and 30% on the remaining years. At BITS Pilani Dubai the offer depends on your course and marks, up to 50% of first-year tuition.

Are the scholarships guaranteed?

No. The percentages shown are indicative. The final scholarship depends on your eligibility, seat availability and the university's approval. We confirm your offer before you commit.

Can I get an education loan?

We guide you on education loan options and share a clear cost breakdown. Approval depends on the lender.

Ready to claim your scholarship?

Scholarship slots are limited for each intake. Share your details, and a counsellor will get in touch with you shortly.
